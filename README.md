Online learning is a method of learning through the internet using electronic devices such as computers, laptops, tablets, and mobile phones.

Students can attend classes, watch video lessons, read notes, complete assignments, and write tests online without physically going to school or college.
10 Easy Points
1. Online learning uses the internet for education.
2. Students can learn from home.
3. It saves time and travelling costs.
4. Classes can be attended using mobile phones or computers.
5. Students can watch recorded video lessons.
6. Teachers can share notes and assignments online.
7. Students can learn at their own speed.
8. Online tests and exams can be conducted.
9. It provides access to different learning resources.
10. It makes education more flexible and convenient.
Advantages
- Saves time and money.
- Learn from anywhere.
- Flexible learning time.
- Easy access to study materials.
- Recorded classes can be watched again.
Disadvantages
- Requires internet access.
- Network problems may occur.
- Less face-to-face interaction.
- Students may get distracted.
- Long screen time can cause discomfort.
Examples of Online Learning Platforms
- Google Classroom
- YouTube
- Coursera
- SWAYAM
Conclusion
Online learning is a modern method of education that helps students learn easily from anywhere using the internet. It makes education flexible, simple, and accessible.
